# November 19th: Planning and Getting Started

Since I accidently forgot about this YSWS, I'm starting like 2 days before the deadline 💀. However, that won't stop me :)

First off, I did some brainstorming on different ideas for about 10 minutes, after which I decided to make a device that can detect whether a mirror is a 2-way mirror.

After deciding roughly what I was going to make, I then proceeded to plan out the the details of my board. More specifically, I was deciding how the board would detect the mirror, how would it let the user know and also a rough sketch of the board on paper.

After that planning/research, I realised that the 2-way mirror worked by having a thinner sheet of reflective material, which would let some light thorugh. This would allow the person on the other side to see throug hit, but not the other person. I also researched that for the most amount of light to go through from the normal person's side, the place of the mirror would have to be dark. That's different to normal mirrors, as they ussually always reflect light. This lead me to me realising I would need the light sensor in some type of cap that would press against the mirror to reduce any external light.

Furthermore, I also then decided I would let the user know if it is a 2-way mirror by ustilising buzzers/vibrations. I would also use LED light to signal if the board was done scanning.
